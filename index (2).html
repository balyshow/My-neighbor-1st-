How It Works

My Neighbors 1ST is built on regular contributions, mutual support, and responsible financial management.

Members can contribute any amount weekly. However, a compulsory contribution of ₦200 every Saturday is required from every member.

Every Saturday is our meeting day at 1:00 PM.

Contributions

Members can contribute any amount they choose each week.

Regular contributions help members develop good saving habits and strengthen financial support within the community.

Loans

Members may apply for financial support through our loan program when they need assistance.

Loans are given without interest.

Loan repayment must be completed by the end of each month.

Members are expected to follow the group's loan rules and repayment arrangements.

Members can contribute any amount they choose together with the additional ₦200 contribution charge.

Members may apply for financial support through the loan program when they need assistance.

Loan conditions and repayment arrangements will be provided by the group.

Saturday Meeting

Meeting Day: Every Saturday

Meeting Time: 1:00 PM

All members are encouraged to attend the Saturday meeting and participate actively in the activities of My Neighbors 1ST.

About My Neighbors 1ST

1. Our Brief History

My Neighbor First is a community-based initiative established with the belief that whatever we do in life, we should always consider our neighbor first.

The organization promotes kindness, unity, financial responsibility, and mutual support among women and men.

My Neighbor First was created to encourage people to save money, create sources of income, acquire useful skills, and become financially independent.

It provides opportunities for skill acquisition and supports women, men, less-privileged people, and members of the community to develop themselves and improve their standard of living.

2. Our Aims and Objectives

  • Encourage regular saving and financial responsibility.
  • Support members in creating sources of income.
  • Promote useful skills acquisition and self-development.
  • Encourage entrepreneurship and financial independence.
  • Support people in improving their standard of living.
  • Promote unity, kindness, and mutual support within the community.

My Neighbor First is committed to empowering women and men, supporting less-privileged people, and creating opportunities for members of the community to develop themselves.

We are particularly committed to empowering women by helping them discover their abilities, develop their talents, build confidence, and understand the important role they can play in their families and communities.

We also encourage discipline, hard work, entrepreneurship, and responsible financial management.

4. Our Belief

My Neighbor First believes that every person has value, potential, and something meaningful to contribute to society.

By putting our neighbors first and supporting one another, we can reduce poverty, create opportunities, strengthen families, and build a more caring and prosperous community.

5. Our Vision and Mission

Vision:
To build a caring and prosperous community where people support one another, create opportunities, and grow together.

Mission:
To empower individuals and families through financial responsibility, skills development, entrepreneurship, mutual support, and opportunities for self-improvement.

Our guiding message:
When we help our neighbor grow, we all grow together.
