How It Works

Members contribute money regularly and can apply for loans when they need financial support.

Contributions can be any amount; an additional ₦200 service charge applies to each contribution.

Contributions are collected every Saturday.

Contributions

Members choose the amount they want to contribute. Each contribution includes the additional ₦200 contribution charge.

Collections take place every Saturday.

Loans

Members may apply for financial support through the group's loan program.

Loan conditions and repayment arrangements are provided by the group and discussed with applicants.

About My Neighbors 1ST

My Neighbors 1ST is a community contribution and loan platform created to help members save regularly and support one another financially.

Why Join My Neighbors 1ST?

  • Contribute money regularly
  • Access financial support when you need it
  • Build a strong saving habit
  • Support and connect with other members

Membership

Become a member and take part in our contribution and loan activities. Members are expected to contribute regularly and follow the group's rules.

Our Services

  • Financial assistance
  • Community support
