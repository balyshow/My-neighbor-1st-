₦

Weekly Contribution

₦200 Compulsory Contribution

Every member is required to pay a compulsory contribution of ₦200 every week. This ₦200 is separate from your personal savings.

₦200 Compulsory

For example, if you are saving ₦20,000, your total weekly payment will be ₦20,200 — ₦20,000 savings plus the compulsory ₦200 contribution.

If you are not saving any amount in a particular week, the ₦200 compulsory contribution must still be paid.

  • ₦200 compulsory contribution every week.
  • Save any amount you choose above ₦200.
  • The ₦200 compulsory contribution is separate from your savings.
  • Collection day is Saturday.

L

Interest-Free Loans

My Neighbors 1ST is designed to support eligible members through an organized interest-free community loan system.

  • Members can apply according to group rules.
  • Loan requests are subject to approval.
  • Members are expected to follow agreed terms.
📅

Loan Repayment

Repayment Deadline: End of the Month

All approved loans must be fully repaid by the end of the month in which the loan repayment is due.

Members are expected to make their loan repayments on time. Please ensure your loan is completely paid before the last day of the month.

⚠️ Important: Failure to repay the loan by the end of the month may affect your eligibility for future loans.

Community Support & Empowerment

Our platform is not only about saving money. We are building a strong and supportive community where members can come together to help one another during important moments in life.

When a member celebrates a special occasion such as a wedding, child dedication, naming ceremony, or other important life event, members may contribute towards supporting and celebrating with the member. Depending on the occasion, each member may contribute an agreed amount, such as ₦2,000 or ₦4,000.

We also encourage skills acquisition and personal development. Members can learn practical skills such as soap making, cosmetics and perfume production, and other income-generating skills.

As part of our commitment to humanity, we also organize courtesy visits to members and people in need, showing care, encouragement, and support within our community.

💛 More Than Saving Building unity, supporting one another, developing skills, and making a positive difference in our community.
