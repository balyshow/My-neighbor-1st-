How It Works

Members contribute money regularly and can apply for loans when they need financial support.

You can contribute any amount, but an additional ₦200 will be added to each contribution.

Contributions are collected every Saturday.

Contributions

Members can contribute any amount they choose, together with the additional ₦200 contribution charge.

Contributions are collected every Saturday.

Loans

Members may apply for financial support through the loan program when they need assistance.

Loan conditions and repayment arrangements will be provided by the group.

About My Neighbors 1ST

My Neighbors 1ST is a community contribution and loan platform created to help members save money regularly and support one another financially.

Why Join My Neighbors 1ST?

  • Contribute money regularly.
  • Get financial support when you need it.
  • Build a strong saving habit.
  • Support and connect with other members.

Membership

Become a member of My Neighbors 1ST and take part in our contribution and loan activities.

Members are expected to contribute regularly and follow the rules of the group.

  • Financial assistance
  • Community support
